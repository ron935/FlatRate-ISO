Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FlatRate ISO ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit flatrateiso.com or contact us through the site. By using the site, you agree to the practices described here.

1.Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Information you provide. When you submit a contact or quote form, we collect the details you enter — typically your name, email address, phone number, business name, and any message or project details you choose to share.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we automatically receive standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and how you interact with the site, gathered through analytics and cookies.

2.Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working properly, understand how visitors use it, and improve our content. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function as intended. We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information.

3.Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third-party providers to operate the site and respond to inquiries. These may include website analytics (such as Google Analytics), bot-protection and security services (such as Cloudflare), and email and hosting providers. These providers process data on our behalf and under their own privacy policies.

4.How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your questions, quote requests, and other inquiries;
  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services and website;
  • Communicate with you about your request or our services;
  • Protect the site against fraud, abuse, and security threats; and
  • Comply with legal obligations.

5.Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only with the service providers who help us operate the site and serve you, when required by law or to respond to lawful requests, or to protect our rights, safety, and property. If our business is involved in a merger or acquisition, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

6.Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7.Your Rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you may ask us to stop contacting you at any time. To make a request, email us at iso@flatrateiso.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

8.Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" above. Your continued use of the site after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10.Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at iso@flatrateiso.com.
